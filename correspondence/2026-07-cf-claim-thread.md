### Anticipated pushback and how to answer it

**"GRIP's download doesn't contain your files."** This is the most likely response and the complaint is framed for it. The answer is **not** to claim GRIP ships the sequences — it doesn't. The answer is:
1. The infringing act is the **reproduction on the user's machine** that GRIP performs and the **redistribution** it enables (export + P2P), of All-Rights-Reserved content, with the owner identifiers removed.
2. GRIP was **built for that purpose and its developers said so in writing before building it** (`evidence/discord/`).
3. CurseForge's IP policy prohibits projects that infringe others' IP — it does not require the infringing bytes to sit inside the addon's zip.

**Do not** answer by re-sending the whole dossier or by claiming source-code copying (disproved) or §1201 circumvention (inapplicable — the data is plaintext on the user's own disk).
