On “Patreon” and “paywall” — how this actually works. The GSE addon is 100% free — the entire addon, distributed free (GSE on CurseForge). Timothy Luke’s Patreon buys two optional creator quality-of-life features only — opening the Editor in combat and mass-exporting sequences — a supporter perk, the way any legitimate addon author funds their work; it gates nothing a user needs. Patreon is purely voluntary tipping — not required, and not monetary (the rights holder says exactly this publicly — see the rebuttal box below). ScaryLarryGames publishes all his current content free on CurseForge. And sequences are not the addon: no sequence is required to use GSE — anyone can write their own anytime on the free version. (A few creators do choose to paywall their own sequences, but a sequence is user content, not the addon, and never necessary to use it.) So House of Macros’ “bypass the paywall / pay the Patreon then import” premise is false at the root — there is no paywall on the addon, and the two Patreon perks have nothing to do with importing sequences. The real issue is copyright: reverse-engineering the free, All-Rights-Reserved addon, stripping the owner identifier, and redistributing content without permission. Free is not free to take.
